❓ FAQ

How much is shipping? · 运费多少？

West Malaysia: free shipping. East Malaysia (Sabah, Sarawak, Labuan): RM13.
西马包邮，东马 RM13。

What happens after I send my order?

I'll check stock for your tumbler and sticker, then send you a payment request. Ready-stock orders get a payment request within 48 hours; for preorder stickers you only pay when the stock arrives.

How long do I have to pay?

Please send your payment receipt within 2 days. Unpaid orders after 2 days will be released.

When will my tumbler be made?

After payment you'll get an estimated week first. The exact live date is confirmed 3–5 days before — if you can't make it, just let me know and we'll reschedule. Your tumbler is made live, and you can choose colours/pattern on the spot.

Do I have to choose a pattern now?

No — you can decide during your live date.

Can I return or cancel?

Every tumbler is 100% handmade, so slight differences in colour and glitter placement are normal. Once an order is confirmed, returns, refunds and cancellations aren't possible — please read step ⑤ carefully before ordering.
